Tokyo · Sunday, December 21

Arrival day, done gently.
Three courses, pick one.

You land at Haneda at 5:10 in the morning. Each course starts at the hotel at 9:00 and has you back by 14:00 for a nap. Little walking, plenty of sitting, and one thing worth remembering.

  • Guide Yuuki
  • Time 9:00–14:00
  • Group family of five
  • Start and end your hotel

Course A · Sit down and see Tokyo

Asakusa & the river

The lowest-walking option. Half of the tour is spent sitting on a boat, so a jet-lagged family can still enjoy it.

  1. 9:00
    Leave the hotelTaxi or train to Asakusa while the streets are still quiet.
  2. 9:20
    Senso-ji temple and Nakamise streetTokyo’s oldest temple. Photos at the big red lantern, incense smoke, first snacks of the trip.
  3. 10:40
    Sumida river cruise (about 35 min)Everyone sits. Skyline, bridges, Tokyo Skytree behind you. Kids can nap.
  4. 11:30
    Hama-rikyu gardensA short, flat walk in a 400-year-old garden. Tea house with a view if energy allows.
  5. 12:30
    LunchTempura or okonomiyaki (kids cook on the hot plate).
  6. 14:00
    Back at the hotelNap time.

Good for Little walking. Works in any energy level. Great photos.

Keep in mind December river wind is cold, bring layers. Boat times to be confirmed for Dec 21.

Course B · Indoor wow, one place only

teamLab Planets & Toyosu

One venue for the whole morning, then lunch next door. Nothing depends on the weather, and the water rooms wake everyone up.

  1. 9:00
    Leave the hotelTrain or taxi to Toyosu.
  2. 9:30
    teamLab Planets (about 2 hours)Barefoot digital art museum. You walk through water and light. Kids love it.
  3. 11:45
    Toyosu Senkyaku BanraiEdo-style food street next to the fish market. Sushi, grilled seafood, wagyu, and easy seats.
  4. 13:00
    Free time or an early returnOptional rooftop hot-spring footbath at the same building.
  5. 14:00
    Back at the hotelNap time.

Good for Weatherproof. Only two moves. The most memorable single spot for kids.

Keep in mind Tickets sell out in December, so we book early. You get wet up to the knees (shorts are provided). Bright, immersive rooms can be a lot for a very tired child.

Course C · Morning light to reset jet lag

Meiji shrine, Harajuku & Shibuya

Daylight is the fastest jet-lag fix. A quiet forest walk first, then the colorful side of Tokyo when the family is awake.

  1. 9:00
    Leave the hotelTrain or taxi to Harajuku.
  2. 9:20
    Meiji Jingu shrineA flat walk through a forest in the middle of the city. Benches along the way. Sunday mornings are calm.
  3. 10:30
    Takeshita streetHarajuku’s teen street opens up. Crepes, character shops, photo booths.
  4. 12:00
    Lunch on OmotesandoSit-down lunch away from the crowd.
  5. 13:00
    Shibuya scramble crossingCross it once, watch it from above, then head back.
  6. 14:00
    Back at the hotelNap time.

Good for No tickets needed. Easy to shorten if anyone fades. Best for jet lag.

Keep in mind Sunday Harajuku gets crowded after 11:00. A bit more walking than A.
