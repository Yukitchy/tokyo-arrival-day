Course A · Sit down and see Tokyo

Asakusa & the river

The lowest-walking option. Half of the tour is spent sitting on a boat, so a jet-lagged family can still enjoy it.

The day

  1. 9:00
    Leave the hotelTaxi or train to Asakusa while the streets are still quiet. About 30 minutes from central Tokyo.
  2. 9:20
    Senso-ji temple and Nakamise streetTokyo’s oldest temple. Photos at the big red lantern, incense smoke, first snacks of the trip. The pier is a 5-minute walk from the temple.
  3. 10:40
    Sumida river cruise, about 40 minutesEveryone sits. Bridges, the skyline, Tokyo Skytree behind you. Kids can nap.
  4. 11:30
    Hama-rikyu gardensA short, flat walk in a 400-year-old garden. Tea house on the pond if energy allows.
  5. 12:30
    LunchSee the three picks below. Tsukiji is a 10-minute walk from the garden gate.
  6. 14:00
    Back at the hotelNap time.

The route

Hotel → Asakusa about 30 min by train. Temple → pier 5 min on foot. Boat 40 min. Garden → Tsukiji 10 min on foot. Tsukiji → hotel about 20 min. Open the route in Google Maps ↗

Good for Little walking. Works at any energy level. Great photos.

Keep in mind December river wind is cold, so bring layers. Boat times for Dec 21 to be confirmed.

Course B · Indoor wow, one place only

teamLab Planets & Toyosu

One venue for the whole morning, then lunch next door. Nothing depends on the weather, and the water rooms wake everyone up.

The day

  1. 9:00
    Leave the hotelTrain or taxi to Toyosu. About 25 minutes from central Tokyo.
  2. 9:30
    teamLab Planets, about 2 hoursBarefoot digital art museum. You walk through water and light, and the rooms react to you.
  3. 11:45
    Toyosu Senkyaku BanraiEdo-style food street beside the fish market. A 10-minute walk from the museum.
  4. 13:00
    Free time or an early returnThere is a rooftop hot-spring footbath in the same building.
  5. 14:00
    Back at the hotelNap time.

The route

Hotel → Shin-Toyosu about 25 min by train. Station → museum 1 min on foot. Museum → food street 10 min on foot. Toyosu → hotel about 25 min. Open the route in Google Maps ↗

Where we eat

Sushi DaiToyosu Market · sushiThe market’s most famous sushi counter. Worth it, but the queue is long, so we go straight there or skip it.Open in Google Maps ↗Daiwa SushiToyosu Market · sushiThe other market classic, usually a shorter wait than Sushi Dai. Chef-picked sets.Open in Google Maps ↗Mekiki Yokocho, Senkyaku BanraiToyosu · food streetTwenty small shops under one roof: rice bowls, grilled seafood, wagyu skewers, ice cream. Easiest with five people and different appetites.Open in Google Maps ↗

Good for Weatherproof. Only two moves all morning. The single most memorable spot for kids.

Keep in mind December tickets sell out, so we book early. You get wet up to the knees, and shorts are provided. Bright immersive rooms can be a lot for a very tired child.

Course C · Morning light to reset jet lag

Meiji shrine, Harajuku & Shibuya

Daylight is the fastest jet-lag fix. A quiet forest walk first, then the colourful side of Tokyo once the family is properly awake.

The day

  1. 9:00
    Leave the hotelTrain or taxi to Harajuku. About 20 minutes from central Tokyo.
  2. 9:20
    Meiji Jingu shrineA flat walk through a forest in the middle of the city, benches along the way. Sunday mornings are calm.
  3. 10:30
    Takeshita streetHarajuku’s teen street opens up. Crepes, character shops, photo booths.
  4. 12:00
    Lunch on OmotesandoSit-down lunch away from the crowd, 10 minutes on foot.
  5. 13:00
    Shibuya scramble crossingCross it once, watch it from above, then head back.
  6. 14:00
    Back at the hotelNap time.

The route

Hotel → Harajuku about 20 min by train. Shrine walk 10 min each way. Harajuku → Omotesando 10 min on foot. Omotesando → Shibuya 12 min on foot. Shibuya → hotel about 20 min. Open the route in Google Maps ↗

Where we eat

Marion CrepesTakeshita street · crepesThe stand that started the Harajuku crepe. Thirty fillings on the photo menu, eaten while walking.Open in Google Maps ↗Maisen AoyamaOmotesando · tonkatsuBreaded pork cutlet in a converted bathhouse. Sit-down, quiet, and easy for children.Open in Google Maps ↗Uobei Shibuya DogenzakaShibuya · conveyor sushiYou order on a tablet and the plates fly to your seat on a rail. Good for No tickets needed. Easy to shorten if anyone fades. Best course for jet lag.
